- **"Didn't have a website":** no live site found (Nov 2026). thesalonsanctuary.co.uk was registered on 30 June 2026 on parking nameservers, and thesalonsanctuary.com on 8 May 2026. Neither loaded from my environment, so **open both on your phone before posting.** If either now shows a real site, the hook has to change.

- **"It tells you what to book":** the finder suggests a starting service from the salon's real Fresha menu. The suggestions are our reading of the menu, labelled on the site as a starting point rather than a quote. The salon should approve them before launch.
- **"Book it, right on their site":** true of the concept's design. In the concept the booking is a clearly labelled demo; live booking needs the salon on Square Appointments (see the website README).
