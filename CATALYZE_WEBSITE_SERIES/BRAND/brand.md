## On-screen caption style (all episodes)

Hanken Grotesk 600, sentence case, white `#ffffff` with a very soft shadow, 2 to 4 words per line, one line at a time, 120 ms fade in and out. One key word per line in `#ff6e00`, sparingly. Kept inside the 9:16 safe zone (clear of the bottom 20% and the right-hand buttons). Moved up or hidden during the website reveal. No bounce, karaoke, emoji or all caps.

## Owner rules that apply to videos and spec sites

No purple, no pill buttons, no invented reviews or numbers, no emoji or glyph icons, no em dashes (avoid en dashes), no scroll-scrubbed or cursor-following motion, no AI slop photos or copy, no "Made with AI" tag. Concept work is labelled as concept work.
