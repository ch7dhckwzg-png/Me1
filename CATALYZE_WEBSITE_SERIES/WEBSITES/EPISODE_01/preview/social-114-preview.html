The oven

After brunch, the fire goes in.

The kitchen switches over in the afternoon: the wood-fired oven comes up to heat and the evening menu starts. Neapolitan pizza cooks fast in a hot oven, so the bases stay light and blistered.

The street

In the middle of the Victoria Quarter.

Victoria Road is New Brighton's street art quarter, and Social 114 sits right in it. Co-owner Robert Jones coordinates the street art on the road; his partner Vic runs the hospitality side.

A short walk down Victoria Road from New Brighton station, towards the prom.
