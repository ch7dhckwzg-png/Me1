# Business research

Researched 22 to 23 November 2026. 25 candidates across Liverpool and the Wirral.

## How each fact was checked

| Check | Method | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website status | Domain registry lookups (Nominet RDAP for .uk, Verisign RDAP for .com) on the obvious name variants, plus web search, plus the "website" field on the business's own booking page where it has one | An unregistered name across every variant, with no site found in search, is strong evidence of no website. A registered domain was then loaded in a real browser where the network allowed it. |
| Social handles | Only handles the business itself links from its own booking page (Fresha partner pages) are marked **verified**. | Instagram blocks unauthenticated profile checks from this environment, so every other handle is **not yet sourced** and must be confirmed on your phone before use. None were guessed. |
| Address, hours, services | Fresha partner pages (written by the business), Food Standards Agency register, local press | Prices are only quoted where the business publishes them. |
| Trading status | Most recent dated source | Marked where uncertain. |

Fresha "lvp" pages are listings Fresha generates for businesses that are not Fresha customers; a blank website field there proves nothing, so those were not used as evidence.

## Gaps to be honest about

- **Builders and gyms:** I found none worth shortlisting. Builders' web presence is mostly on Checkatrade and FMB, and those I found already have sites. Gyms found were chains or had sites. Happy to do a dedicated pass if you want one in the five.
- **Pet groomers:** the two without websites are home-based (nothing to film from the street).
- **Photos:** I could not reach Google Maps or Instagram, so the shortlist has no business photos except screenshots of the two sites that loaded. Links to each business's listing are in the shortlist for you to look.
- **Instagram handles:** only 6 are verified (linked by the business itself). The rest need a 10-second check on your phone; I've listed exactly which.
