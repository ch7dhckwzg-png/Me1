    What the setup can do

    From the environment audit. The parts marked in red need a decision from you before the stage that uses them.

    Social, via PostZen

    • Instagram @catalyze.uk connected
    • TikTok @catalyze.uk connected
    • Facebook not connected yet
    • Drafts, collab invites, first comments, analytics
    • Instagram can't put user tags on reels through the API, so reels tag the business with an @mention, plus an optional collab invite

    Build and video

    • Node, Astro and Tailwind installable, real Chromium for checks and screen recording
    • FFmpeg and Remotion for the edit and caption burn-in
    • Previews run inside my workspace only; a shareable preview needs your OK and a host (Netlify works today, Cloudflare needs access)

    Voice and maps

    • ElevenLabs and OpenAI voices: no account or key here yet
    • Satellite and map imagery blocked; I'd lead with your own landmark footage, or a Google Earth Studio clip you export with its attribution

    Decisions waiting on you

    1. Pick five businesses and their order (above)
    2. The tagline "Elevate. Illuminate. Activate." clashes with your site rule against "elevate". Keep it, change it, or leave it out?
    3. Voice provider: ElevenLabs recommended (it gives word timings for the captions)
    4. Connect your Facebook Page in PostZen if you want Facebook drafts
    5. Rotate the PostZen key; it was shared in the chat
