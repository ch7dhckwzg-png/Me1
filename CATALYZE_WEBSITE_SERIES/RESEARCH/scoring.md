## Scoring notes

- **Commercial potential** leans on review volume, price level and whether there's an owner who could buy a site (charities, volunteer cafés and home businesses score low).
- **Visual potential** counts what you can film from the street, plus how good the product looks on screen.
- **Local appeal** is how many people in the audience will recognise the place.
- Fleur Ashley ranks 10th despite having a site because the current site is a stock theme; it's the "outdated website" case in the series, if you want one.
