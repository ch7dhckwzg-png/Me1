FAQs

Anything else, call us on 0151 496 0123.

Do I need to book?

No, walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free. Booking ahead means no wait, especially on Saturdays.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes, under-12s are £16. An adult needs to stay with them.

How do I pay?

Card or cash in the shop.

Is there parking?

On-street parking outside, and we're two minutes' walk from the train station. Step-free entrance.
